Upload trophy images here (PNG, WebP, JPG or SVG). In the player editor Honours List, use one line per award: Title | Count | Years | Image URL (optional). Example path: assets/trophies/cup.webp. An HTTPS image URL also works. Missing or broken images use the SVG trophy icon. Admin edits are saved locally in the browser.

Club Honours page: upload champions.webp for Malaysian Champions and fa-cup.webp for the FA Cup. Until uploaded, local SVG trophy icons are displayed.
